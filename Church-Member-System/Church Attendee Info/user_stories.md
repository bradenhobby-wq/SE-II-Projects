## User Stories
### US-2.1: Update General Information via Form
**As a** guest with access to the internet
**I want to** easily update my general information on the online form
**So that** the church has updated information and can support me to the best of their abilities

**Priority:** P1
**Independent test:** Fill out the form and see if the general information was stored properly.
**Acceptance scenarios:** see ### US-2.1 under Acceptance
Criteria

### US-2.2: Church Staff Views Church Member's/Guest's General Information
**As a** worker at the church
**I want to** be able to view the general information of anyone in the database
**So that** the church can support them more effectively

**Priority:** P1
**Independent test:** A member of the church staff can see the general information of anyone
**Acceptance scenarios:** see ### US-2.2 under Acceptance
Criteria
---

### US-2.3: Church Staff Edits Member's Information
**As a** worker at the church who has been told to update someone's information
**I want to** be able to add the member's information on our database
**So that** the church can support them more effectively

**Priority:** P2
**Independent test:** A member of the church staff can edit a member's profile
**Acceptance scenarios:** see ### US-2.3 under Acceptance
Criteria
